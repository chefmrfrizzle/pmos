# Research method

The deterministic pipeline starts with first-party pages, robots rules, sitemaps and structured metadata, then uses reputable public registries. Every assertion becomes a claim; missing facts remain missing. Snapshots are content-addressed, changes create review work, and history is never overwritten. Jobs are bounded, checkpointed, retry with backoff, and isolate failures by institution.

Machine-written or generic pages are not treated as inherently authoritative. Source specificity, named authorship, primary documents, corroboration, freshness, and exact supporting passages contribute separately to confidence.

Private source rows generate provenance-linked candidate claims only. Deterministic resolution auto-links exact evidence; ambiguous names stay separate and enter review.

Official-web research validates public HTTP(S) targets and every redirect against private/local addresses, fails closed when robots rules are unavailable, rate-limits per host, limits response size and content type, and records bounded job outcomes. A successful fetch creates evidence; only predicate-specific matching can create a `SUPPORTED` claim. `VERIFIED` remains a human promotion.

A supported identity assertion creates four linked records: the immutable claim, ranked `SourceDocument`, bounded `EvidencePassage`, and `ClaimEvidence` link with directness. Page-level retrieval is insufficient. Passage extraction must find the institution’s identity tokens in the title or bounded body context; otherwise the job enters review. The passage, offsets/section, document hash, publisher independence group, retrieval time, and extractor version remain inspectable.

Official-institution homepages are S1 self-description. They can support public identity but cannot independently verify ownership/control, regulatory standing, transaction authority, comparable AUM, reputation, or an institutional relationship.

GLEIF is treated as S2 recognized market infrastructure. Search results create `RegistryIdentifierCandidate` records and structured evidence passages; result order never determines identity. Deterministic comparison produces `PROBABLE_MATCH`, `POSSIBLE_MATCH`, `CONFLICT`, or `REQUIRES_REVIEW`. No candidate populates `LegalIdentifier` automatically. Acceptance requires a probable registry match, separately supported official identity evidence, maker-checker approval, and a new specialist-verified claim while preserving the candidate claim.

Review throughput is risk-based, not approval-rate-based. The queue sorts deterministic conflicts and higher-confidence probable matches ahead of unresolved low-information rows, but priority never changes the proof threshold. Review packets omit raw source rows, use optimistic versions to reject stale decisions, require evidence scoped to one of the identities, and bind maker and checker to the same evidence digest. Rejecting, deferring, or marking a conflict preserves the candidates and history.

Dossiers expose incompleteness explicitly. A claim without an exact passage is `UNLINKED`; a fact beyond its predicate-specific interval is `STALE`; multiple non-rejected values for a material single-value predicate create a potential-conflict warning; and recorded material conflicts block readiness until resolved by a specialist. These are review signals, not machine conclusions about beneficial ownership, authority, or wrongdoing.

First-party source expansion is a separate bounded stage. PMOS inspects sanitized homepage links, keeps only credential-free URLs on the same registrable host, removes tracking parameters, and classifies likely annual reports, regulatory disclosures, governance pages, fund-structure pages, strategy material, private-client pages, legal pages, and locations. A classified link is only a `ResearchSourceCandidate`.

Retrieval rechecks robots rules, redirects, DNS safety, content type, and size. Successful HTML becomes a content-addressed S1 document snapshot. Deterministic keyword windows may create exact `ResearchPassageCandidate` records for the case predicate, but not claims. Unsupported PDFs, robots denials, oversized content, and retryable failures leave the active queue with explicit ledgered outcomes rather than being repeatedly fetched or silently discarded.

Official PDFs use a separate subprocess extractor. The parent bounds downloaded bytes, validates the `%PDF-` signature, strips proxy/credential environment variables, applies CPU/file/process limits, enforces a wall-clock timeout, and caps extractor output. The worker rejects encrypted documents and excessive page counts, bounds text per page and document, and returns page-numbered normalized text with hashes. PDF passage candidates retain the exact page number. Scanned or malformed documents remain explicit non-extractable/failed outcomes and are never treated as negative evidence.

Reverification selects only retrieved sources whose latest snapshot or change event is older than the configured interval. It repeats the same network and parsing controls, preserves the earlier snapshot, and records an immutable comparison. Identical content becomes `UNCHANGED`; changed content receives deterministic similarity and token-set metrics plus an exact private diff preview in the review API. No existing claim, passage decision, check, or score is changed automatically. Failed reverification preserves the last known source status and writes a ledgered failure outcome.

Passage adjudication is assertion-specific. The maker must choose a substantive value that occurs verbatim after whitespace/case normalization inside the exact passage. Independent approval must use the same value and evidence chain. A changed candidate state, invented/paraphrased value, self-approval, altered approval value, broken evidence chain, or material contradiction fails closed. Approved S1 passages create `SUPPORTED`, not specialist-verified, claims and never complete a diligence check automatically.

Supported claims are routed to diligence procedures by exact predicate/fact-class equality. Routing is a recommendation, not evidence attachment. A scoped reviewer must accept the route before PMOS links the claim to the check. The attachment moves a new check to `EVIDENCE_COLLECTED`; source independence, unresolved conflicts, maker-checker approval, exceptions, and final readiness are evaluated separately.
