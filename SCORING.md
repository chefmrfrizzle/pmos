# Scoring

PMOS keeps capital access, asset access, network leverage, institutional influence, wealth relevance, private-asset fit, contactability, relationship, evidence, freshness, conversion, and strategic counterparty scores distinct. Scores are bounded 0–100 and expose each input, weight, and contribution. Deterministic strategy remains primary; a local interpretable conversion model is gated until a meaningful labeled sample exists and must display sample size and uncertainty.
