# Private Markets OS synthetic demonstration

The command center presents a fictional contemporary artwork moving through authority, provenance, attribution, restitution, export, collector matching, deal-room, closing, audit, and graph-update gates. Every person, collector, entity, relationship, value, and transaction detail is synthetic. It contains no Sotheby's or user-provided intelligence.

Message: PMOS converts fragmented private-market evidence and institutional relationships into a transaction-ready operating layer while preserving specialist judgment.
